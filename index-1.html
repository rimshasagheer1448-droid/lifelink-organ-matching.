How it works

Five stages, one optimal result.

1 · REGISTRATION

Capture records

Donor and patient data enter the registry.

2 · VALIDATION

Check eligibility

Blood group, HLA and location are validated.

3 · GRAPH BUILDER

Build the graph

Donors D and patients P become vertices; an edge means Compatible(d, p). No edges within a side: a bipartite graph.

4 · MATCHING ENGINE

Maximize matches

Augmenting paths (the idea behind Hopcroft–Karp) find the largest set of pairs, processing the most urgent patients first.

5 · RESULT

Notify pairs

Matched pairs are reported for clinical review and notification.
